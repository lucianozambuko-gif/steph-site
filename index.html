About

A storyteller at heart. I use words, ideas and conversations to help brands and people connect in ways that mean something.

My work sits where storytelling meets strategy. I create content, host and moderate events, design communication campaigns and community programs, and help brands shape stories people care about.

People know me for a thoughtful approach, a sharp wit and a knack for finding the story under the surface. I believe every brand, event and person has a story worth telling, and worth telling well.

Outside client work, I'm drawn to literature, mental-health awareness and community building: spaces where people can learn, connect and share ideas.

Run of show

Three hours, planned to the minute, and still loose enough to breathe

Every evening follows an event flow I script: structured enough to keep the energy up, open enough that the best moments come from the floor. This is the flow for the reality-TV edition.

Interactive formats keep readers and non-readers alike in the room. There are prompt cards on every table, hot-take icebreakers, and polls where guests physically pick a side: Team Lyrics Matter or Team Vibes Matter.

Community design
Safe Space · Women & girls community model

A community where women feel seen.

A community model and management plan for SAFE SPACE: a WhatsApp-first network for women and girls to learn, connect, access mentorship and get practical support, designed to become a sustainable leadership pipeline.

Women don't need another lecture. They need a community where they feel seen.

  • Her MindMental health & wellness

    Burnout, anxiety, grief, loneliness and imposter syndrome, held in anonymous confessions and “How are you really doing?” check-ins.

  • Her MoneyFinancial literacy

    Budgeting, saving, side hustles, salary negotiation and freelancing, with female entrepreneurs, financial advisors and women in tech.

  • Her PurposeCareer & development

    A monthly Career Clinic where members submit CVs, cover letters and career questions for review. LinkedIn, leadership and networking.

  • Her VoiceConfidence & expression

    Personal branding, public speaking and content creation, built through writing prompts, storytelling challenges and opinion discussions.

  • Her CommunityRelationships & sisterhood

    Friendships, family, dating and boundaries: building the support systems that make everything else possible.

Signature activity

Dear Younger Me

Once a month, members send in anonymous letters to their younger selves. I read selected entries aloud and the community discusses them. It's built on the three things that keep people in a community: stories, vulnerability and participation.
